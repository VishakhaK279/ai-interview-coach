# Data Engineering Interview Scoring Guide

This scoring guide scores one answer to one interview question (0–100). Judge the quality and evidence of that response, not the candidate’s overall readiness across the entire job description. Assess only role skills meaningfully tested by the question; do not lower the score for unrelated skills the question did not give the candidate a chance to demonstrate. Use in combination with the relevant rubric criteria and benchmark Q&A.

## 0–20: Limited Evidence
- The answer is a non-answer, off-topic, or offers no relevant evidence.
- It only names a tool or concept without explaining its use.
- Do not use this band merely because unrelated job-description skills are absent.

## 21–40: Basic Competence
- The answer partially addresses the question and shows some relevant familiarity.
- It lacks important detail about the candidate’s approach, decisions, or contribution.
- The example may be generic, mostly theoretical, or unclear about the result.

## 41–60: Solid Practitioner
- The answer gives a relevant, practical example and explains at least part of the candidate’s approach.
- It includes useful details for the skill or scenario tested by this question.
- Some context, technical depth, or outcome may be missing.

## 61–80: Strong Candidate
- The answer is specific and well structured, with clear ownership and sound technical reasoning relevant to the question.
- It explains meaningful implementation details, trade-offs, validation, challenges, or results as appropriate.
- Minor gaps in depth or evidence remain.

## 81–100: Exceptional Candidate
- The answer directly and thoroughly addresses the question with strong, credible evidence.
- It demonstrates excellent ownership, technical judgment, and relevant detail; quantified impact strengthens the answer when applicable but is not mandatory for every question.
- It may explain trade-offs, verification, lessons learned, or stakeholder impact where relevant.
- Do not infer senior-level readiness from a single answer.
